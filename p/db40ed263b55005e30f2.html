What these words mean

Volume Profile = where most shares were bought and sold in the last 60 trading days. Prices where many people traded often act as a floor (the price stops falling there) or a ceiling (it stops rising).

POC (Point of Control) = the ONE price where the most shares traded. The strongest level.

VAL (Value Area Low) = the bottom of the busy zone (70% of all trading happened between VAL and VAH). Often a floor.

VAH (Value Area High) = the top of the busy zone. Often a ceiling.

How the robot uses it: BUY at the nearest VAL or POC under the price, but only after the stock stops falling (while it still falls it says WAIT). SELL just under the next VAH above the price, only if you make 4%+. If the price is already above the VAH, there is no ceiling above it, so no Volume Profile sell. It is a second price next to the bot's own; the Floor report checks which one works better.
