How the alert number is chosen

A "big drop" = 2 times the stock's normal daily move over the last 3 months, rounded to the nearest 0.5%.
Normal day = the average size of one day's move (up or down) over the last 3 months.
The robot calculates the numbers again every month, so they follow how the stock moves now.
Note: in the last 3 months Amazon moved less than Nvidia, so its alert is lower than Nvidia's, not higher.

How the alert works

Stocks in the US and Canada (Nvidia, Google, Microsoft, Amazon, Shopify, Palantir, AMD, Applied Materials): the robot watches the US price, including the pre-market (from 4:00 AM), because the US moves first. You get ONE alert with both prices: US dollars and Canadian dollars.
Shopify: both SHOP (US) and TSX:SHOP. They move the same way; the US one is watched first.
Bitcoin + FBTC: ONE alert, measured on Bitcoin itself (it trades 24 hours, 7 days). The FBTC price is shown in the same alert.
Dollarama: Canada only (TSX), watched during TSX hours.
Stocks you already own get this alert too, marked "you own it".
At most one alert per stock per day, on your phone and your PC. It tells you to look, not to buy: you decide.
